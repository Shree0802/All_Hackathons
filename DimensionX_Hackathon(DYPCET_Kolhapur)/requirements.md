
# Requirements Document
## Project Title
Mahadbt Scholarship Tracking & Follow-Up Automation System

## Team
DynamicA4X

## Problem Statement
Colleges manage hundreds of Mahadbt scholarship applications every academic year. The process is mostly manual, involving Excel sheets, registers, and repeated follow-ups. This leads to missed deadlines, errors, lack of transparency, and higher rejection rates.

## Objective
To build a centralized, automated system that tracks scholarship application status, sends timely reminders, and reduces manual workload for college staff.

## Target Users
- Students applying for Mahadbt scholarships
- College scholarship office administrators
- Department-level staff

## Functional Requirements
- Student data collection through a single submission
- Centralized storage of scholarship application data
- Automatic tracking of application status
- Identification of pending documents or actions
- Automated reminders to students and administrators
- Admin dashboard to monitor all applications
- Status visibility for students

## Non-Functional Requirements
- Secure authentication and access control
- Scalable to support multiple colleges and scholarships
- Reliable and available during peak submission periods
- Easy to use with minimal training

## Constraints
- Dependent on information available from students and college records
- Requires internet connectivity
- No direct modification of government Mahadbt portal

## Assumptions
- Students provide correct and complete information
- College staff regularly monitor the dashboard
